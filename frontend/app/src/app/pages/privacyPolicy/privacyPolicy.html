Privacy Policy

Last updated: [01/10/2026]

PEP is a school project with no commercial purpose. It is not intended to be hosted publicly. Data is used only to display content in the app and to demonstrate how it could work. No real users are expected: only test data should be entered.

Data Collection

We collect the following data:

  • Profile: username, email address, hashed password
  • Content: items (name, description, state, category), posts, groups and their descriptions
  • Private messages
  • Lending history
  • Technical data: refresh token, IP address

Data Usage

  • Email: identifies your account and is used to send confirmation emails, password resets and alerts.
  • Username: lets other users (friends, family, colleagues) identify you.
  • Items, posts and private messages: make the lending system work.
  • Technical data (refresh token, IP address): keeps you logged in and allows us to monitor the service.

Other users can see your username and your posts. Your email address and private messages are never shown to other users, except the recipient of a message.

Data Sharing

Connection data (such as IP addresses) is used to monitor the service with Grafana. Grafana is hosted locally as part of the project: no data is sent to any third party.

Data Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. All data is deleted at the end of the project [or: after X days of inactivity].

User Rights

You can delete your profile, items and groups at any time. Private messages related to a trade in progress remain available until that trade is completed. After that, [they are deleted / they are anonymized]. When your account is deleted, [your posts and lending history are deleted / anonymized].

To exercise your rights, contact us at [contact email].

Security Measures

Passwords are stored hashed using [bcrypt / argon2] and are never stored in plain text. Authentication relies on [JWT access and refresh tokens]. [Communications are encrypted with HTTPS.]

This project is not intended to be put online or commercialized. We cannot be held responsible for any security failure if it is used outside the scope it was made for: a local school project.
