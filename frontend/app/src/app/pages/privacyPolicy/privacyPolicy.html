Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26/10/2026

PEP is a school project with no commercial purpose. It is not intended to be hosted publicly. Data is used only to display content in the app and to demonstrate how it could work. No real users are expected: only test data should be entered.

Data Collection

We collect the following data:

  • Profile: username, email address, hashed password
  • Content: items (name, description, state, category), posts, groups and their descriptions
  • Private messages
  • Lending history
  • Technical data: refresh token, API keys (hashed), IP address (not stored)

Data Usage

  • Email: identifies your account and can be used to log in. No emails are sent.
  • Username: lets other users (friends, family, colleagues) identify you.
  • Items, posts and private messages: make the lending system work.
  • Technical data: the refresh token, kept in an httpOnly cookie, keeps you logged in. The IP address is only used in memory to rate limit requests.

Other users can see your username, your items and the posts you share with their groups. Your items are also visible through the public API. Your email address and private messages are never shown to other users, except the recipient of a message.

Data Sharing

The service is monitored with Prometheus and Grafana, which only record technical metrics (request counts, response times, status codes) and no personal data. Grafana is hosted locally as part of the project: no data is sent to any third party.

Data Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your private messages are deleted and the rest of your data is hidden but kept in the database. All data is deleted at the end of the project or after a database cleanup.

User Rights

You can delete your profile, items and the groups you own at any time. When you delete your account, loans that have not started yet are cancelled and the groups you own are handed over to their longest-standing member.

To exercise your rights, contact us at rshin@student.42lyon.fr.

Security Measures

Passwords are stored hashed using argon2 and are never stored in plain text. Authentication relies on JWT access and refresh tokens. Communications are encrypted with HTTPS.

This project is not intended to be put online or commercialized. We cannot be held responsible for any security failure if it is used outside the scope it was made for: a local school project.
